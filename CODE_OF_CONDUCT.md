# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- owning mistakes, apologizing to those affected and learning from them

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@Walter-Dalla](https://github.com/Walter-Dalla), through GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) flow or privately through the contact on the maintainer's profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and the reporter's identity is kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
